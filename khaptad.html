The Destination

A dive into the untouched far west

Known for its natural beauty and biological diversity, the Khaptad area can be reached through Doti, Achham, Bajhang, and Bajura districts of Nepal. Beyond its natural surroundings, Khaptad is renowned as a sacred and spiritual destination, having been the home of the Hindu sage Khaptad Swami, also known as Khaptad Baba. He spent many years meditating in the region, studying medicinal herbs, and seeking a life of simplicity and tranquility. A designated area of the park was reserved for meditation and remains a peaceful place for reflection and spiritual practice. For those seeking spiritual awakening, solitude, and time away from the noise of everyday life, Khaptad offers a rare opportunity to slow down, reconnect with yourself, and find peace in the mountains.

Khaptad National Park, established in 1984, is one of the main attractions of the region and is covered by beautiful oak and rhododendron forests. At elevations reaching around 12,000 feet, the park is home to diverse flora and fauna, including bears, leopards, common langurs, musk deer, hundreds of bird species, and a remarkable variety of medicinal herbs. Walking through these quiet forests, open meadows, and peaceful mountain landscapes offers more than an adventure—it creates space to reflect, breathe, and spend time with yourself.
