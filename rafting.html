River Rafting

Ride the currents of the Mahakali River through rugged valleys and remote landscapes, experiencing an unforgettable blend of adventure, nature, and Far Western Nepalese culture.

  • Duration half-day/full-day or 5 days
  • Max Altitude ~197 m
  • Region Karnali Province, West Nepal
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Best Season Oct-mid June
  • Price On Request
The Destination

Rafting through the fierce currents of the Mahakali river

The Mahakali River offers an exhilarating way to experience the wild landscapes of Sudurpaschim Province. Flowing through the far western edge of Nepal, the river winds past rugged hills, forests, remote villages, and dramatic river valleys, creating a unique setting for an adventure away from Nepal's more crowded rafting destinations. Its flowing waters and untouched surroundings make the Mahakali an exciting destination for travellers seeking both adventure and a deeper connection with the landscapes of Far West Nepal.

At Kafali Adventure, we see Mahakali rafting as more than a rush through the rapids. It is a chance to explore the natural beauty of the region, experience local communities along the river, and appreciate a landscape that remains largely untouched by mass tourism. From the sound of rushing water to the views of the surrounding hills, every stretch of the Mahakali brings a different side of Sudurpaschim to life.

Trip Highlights

  • Raft one of Nepal's most scenic western rivers
  • Perfect for both beginners and experienced rafters
  • Exciting Class II–IV rapids depending on section
  • Professional rafting guides and safety kayakers

Day by Day

Your itinerary (full-day package)

A suggested plan for the full-day package.

  1. Day 1 : 7am

    Depart from Mahendranagar

    Depart from Mahendranagar and travel to the rafting point: Parshuram / Chuka

  2. Day 1 : 8am

    Rafting first half

    After safety and gear briefing, raft all the way from Chuka to halfway point and have lunch around 12pm and rest

  3. Day 1 : 2pm

    Rafting second half

    After lunch, raft from the halfway point to BOOM

  4. Day 1: 5pm

    Arrival at BOOM

    Reach around 5pm and freshen up and have snacks and dinner and head back to Mahendranagar

Day by Day

Your itinerary ( 5 days package)

A suggested plan for the 5 days package.

  1. Day 1

    Travel to Pancheshwor

    Depart from Mahendranagar and travel to Pancheshwor via Tanakpur and have lunch on the way. Overnight stay at Pancheshwor

  2. Day 2

    Rafting from Pancheshwor to Dimbarghat

    Raft from Pancheshwor to Dimbarghar and check in stay the night at Dimbarghat

  3. Day 3

    Rafting from Dimbarghat to Rupal

    Arrive at Rupal and then have dinner and stay the night at Rupal

  4. Day 4

    Rafting from Rupal to Parigaun / Chuka

    Arrive at Parigaun / Chuka and stay the night at Parigaun / Chuka

  5. Day 5

    Raft from Parigaun / Chuka to BOOM

    Arrive at BOOM, freshen up and have snacks and head back to Mahendranagar

Note:We recommend keeping one extra day in your itinerary as a buffer to accommodate unforeseen circumstances such as weather, road conditions, delays, or other unexpected changes.
