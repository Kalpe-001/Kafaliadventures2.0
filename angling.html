Spend four days beside Nepal's rivers, enjoying a relaxed angling experience with riverside camping, all meals, and essential fishing equipment included.

The Destination

A dive into the untouched far west

Leave the busy trails behind and spend four days surrounded by the natural beauty of Nepal's rivers. Our angling experience is designed for those looking to slow down, cast a line, and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of the outdoors, with riverside camping and freshly prepared meals throughout the journey.

Whether you're an experienced angler or simply looking for a different way to experience Nepal, this trip combines fishing, camping, and time in nature into a relaxed wilderness adventure. Spend your days by the river, enjoy the surrounding landscape, and experience Nepal at a slower and more immersive pace.

Trip Highlights

  • Spend your days surrounded by the beautiful rivers and natural landscapes
  • Enjoy dedicated time for angling in a peaceful setting
  • Stay overnight in camping sites surrounded by nature
  • Experience a more immersive side of Nepal in a relaxed pace
Day by Day

Your itinerary

A suggested 4 day plan. Every departure can be tailored. You can talk to us about custom dates, pacing and add-ons.

  1. Day 1

    Kathmandu / Delhi to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi

    Arrive at Mahendrangar and then stay the night.

  2. Day 2

    Mahendranagar to Pancheshwor

    Travel to Pancheshwor from Mahendranagar via India and then stay the night

  3. Day 3-4

    Enjoy Angling

    Enjoy angling in Pancheshwor

  4. Day 5

    Travel back to Mahendranagar

    Drive back to Mahendranagar and stay the night. Head out to your drop-off point the next day

  5. Opt Day

    Optional safari

    A jungle safari at Shuklaphanta Wildlife Reserve before you depart.

Note:Extra day with extra cost if the part wants to spend more time angling and it includes travel cost, camp accomodation, rod, cords and meals

The Details

What's included

Inclusions

  • All ground transportation: private jeep transfers / micro-bus from pick-up to drop-off on return
  • Accommodation in hotels, guesthouses and tea houses on a twin-sharing basis
  • Homestay or basic lodges during the cycling (Budar, Jogbudha, Malas)
  • Three meals a day — breakfast, lunch and dinner (Nepali, Indian and local), with tea at times
  • Licensed, English-speaking guide
  • First aid kit carried by the guide
  • All local taxes and administrative fees
  • Contingency arrangements for emergencies (weather delays, medical support)

Exclusions

  • International airfare and Nepal entry visa fees
  • Domestic flights not specified in the itinerary
  • Travel and rescue / medical insurance (mandatory)
  • Personal cycling equipment and clothing
  • Drinks and bottled water
  • Tips for guides and drivers
  • Personal expenses and any optional activities
  • Extra costs from delays beyond our control (weather, flight cancellations, etc.)
  • Anything not listed under “Inclusions”
