Cycling

Cycle through the scenic Chure foothills, winding past forests, villages, and rolling landscapes while enjoying fresh air, peaceful surroundings, and an unforgettable countryside adventure filled with nature and exploration.

  • Duration 8 Days
  • Max Altitude ~1,386m
  • Region Far West Nepal
  • Difficulty Moderate
  • Best Season Oct - June
  • Price On Request
The Destination

A dive into the untouched far west

Ride through the scenic foothills of the Chure Range, where winding trails, quiet forests, and rural landscapes create an exciting escape from the city. The route takes you across a mix of forest paths, gentle hills, and countryside roads, offering a refreshing way to experience the natural beauty of southern Nepal.

Perfect for adventure seekers and cycling enthusiasts, this journey combines outdoor exploration with the peaceful atmosphere of the Chure foothills. Enjoy changing landscapes, local villages, forested trails, and panoramic views as you pedal through one of Nepal’s lesser-explored natural regions.

Trip Highlights

  • Explore scenic forest trails and quiet rural roads
  • Cycle through the peaceful foothills of the Chure Rang
  • Enjoy panoramic views of forests, hills, and surrounding landscapes
  • Discover local villages and experience the countryside at a relaxed pace
  • Experience an adventurous day surrounded by nature and fresh mountain air
Day by Day

Your itinerary

A suggested 8 day plan. Every departure can be tailored. You can talk to us about custom dates, pacing and add-ons.

  1. Day 1

    Kathmandu / Delhi to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi

    Flight from Kathmandu to Dhangadhi, or drive from Delhi to the border and stay at Mahendranagar. Travel by private car or rented vehicle.

  2. Day 2

    Drive with mountain bikes to the foothill

    Arrival at foothills and then start cycling. Cycle uphill till Malas and nightstay at Malas

  3. Day 3

    Cycling from Malas to Pooranpani

    Arrival at Pooranpani and night stay at Pooranpani. Cycle down from Puranpani to Jogubudha. Nigh stay at Jogbudha

  4. Day 4

    Cycling from Jogbudha to Budar

    Cycle and have lunch and stay the night at Budar.

  5. Day 5

    Cycle from Budar to Sahajpur.

    Cycle from Budar to Sahajpur and stay the night in Sahajpur in tented camps.

  6. Day 6

    Cycle from Sahajpur to Khanidanda

    Cycle to Khanidanda both uphill and downhill and stay the night in Khanidanda.

  7. Day 7

    Drive back to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi

    Begin the return journey, and stay the night at Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi.

  8. Day 8

    Travel back to your destination

    Travel back to your starting point (airport / border) dropoff

  9. Opt Day

    Optional safari

    A jungle safari at Shuklaphanta Wildlife Reserve before you depart.

Note: One buffer day is included to allow for unforeseen circumstances such as weather delays.

The Details

What's included

Inclusions

  • All ground transportation: private jeep transfers / micro-bus from pick-up to drop-off on return
  • Accommodation in hotels, guesthouses and tea houses on a twin-sharing basis
  • Homestay or basic lodges during the cycling (Budar, Jogbudha, Malas)
  • Three meals a day — breakfast, lunch and dinner (Nepali, Indian and local), with tea at times
  • Licensed, English-speaking guide
  • First aid kit carried by the guide
  • All local taxes and administrative fees
  • Contingency arrangements for emergencies (weather delays, medical support)

Exclusions

  • International airfare and Nepal entry visa fees
  • Domestic flights not specified in the itinerary
  • Travel and rescue / medical insurance (mandatory)
  • Personal cycling equipment and clothing
  • Drinks, bottled water, hot showers and Wi-Fi where charged
  • Tips for guides and drivers
  • Personal expenses and any optional activities
  • Extra costs from delays beyond our control (weather, flight cancellations, etc.)
  • Anything not listed under “Inclusions”
