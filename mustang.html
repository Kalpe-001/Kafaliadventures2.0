The Destination

A dive into the Mystic Mustang

Mustang is a land of dramatic landscapes, ancient villages, and timeless Himalayan culture. Hidden behind the towering Annapurna and Dhaulagiri ranges, this remote region is known for its windswept valleys, striking cliffs, barren mountains, and centuries-old monasteries. The ancient Kingdom of Lo gives Mustang a distinctive cultural identity, with Tibetan-influenced traditions, architecture, and communities that have endured for generations.

At Kafali Adventure, we see Mustang as a journey into a different side of Nepal where rugged mountain trails meet rich history and living traditions. From the walled city of Lo Manthang to peaceful mountain villages and ancient caves carved into cliffs, every trail reveals another piece of Mustang's remarkable heritage.

Day by Day

Your itinerary

A suggested 6 day plan. Every departure can be tailored — talk to us about custom dates, pacing and add-ons.

  1. Day 1

    Kathmandu / Delhi to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi

    Come in from Kathmandu / Delhi to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi and stay the night in town

  2. Day 2

    Drive from Mahendranagar to Pokhara

    Have breakfast and then head to Pokhara. Stay the night at Pokhara and enjoy the lakeside

  3. Day 3

    Drive from Pokhara to Jomsom

    Have a scenic drive from Pokhara to Jomsom and stay the night at Jomsom

  4. Day 4

    Full day excursion at Mustang

    Visit to Muktinath, Kagbeni, apple farms and nearby lakes and villages.

  5. Day 5

    Descend from Mustang to Pokhara

    Arrive at Pokhara and stay the night in Pokhara

  6. Day 6

    Drive back to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi

    Drive back from Pokhara and descend to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi

  7. Opt Day

    Extra one day to Visit upper Mustang ( Lomanthang)

    Optional: A scenic ride to Lomanthang and visit the walled city, monasteries and the Nepal-China border

  8. Opt Day

    Explore Lumbini

    Optional: Explore Lumbini the birthplace of Buddha and the various monasteries and temples.

    9. Note:We recommend keeping one extra day in your itinerary as a buffer to accommodate unforeseen circumstances such as weather, road conditions, delays, or other unexpected changes.
