Shivalik Hiking

Explore the Shivalik Hills of Nepal's Chure Range, where forested ridges, river valleys, and diverse wildlife create an authentic journey through the country's southern landscapes.

  • Region Far West Nepal

  • Price On Request
The Destination

A dive into the untouched far west

The Shivalik Hills form the southernmost range of the Himalayas, stretching across Nepal's Terai and rising gradually into the rugged landscapes of the Chure region. Covered in forests, river valleys, and rolling ridges, the area offers a quieter and less-explored side of Nepal, with diverse ecosystems and landscapes shaped by centuries of natural change. Within just a four to five-hour walk, travellers can experience noticeable changes in weather, altitude, and vegetation, making the Shivaliks a remarkable showcase of Nepal's natural diversity.

The Chure landscape is home to an extraordinary mix of flora, where forests of Sal trees meet pine-covered slopes and lowland vegetation gradually converges with plants found at higher elevations. This unique transition creates an exceptional variety of ecosystems within a relatively short distance. Exploring the Shivaliks offers travellers the opportunity to walk through these changing landscapes, experience nature's remarkable diversity up close, and discover a quieter side of Nepal beyond its famous Himalayan trails.

Trip Highlights

  • Explore the forested hills and rolling ridges of the Shivalik Range
  • Walk through the diverse forests and valleys of the Chure landscape
  • Discover hidden streams, riverbeds, and seasonal waterfalls
  • Spot native birds and wildlife along peaceful forest trails
  • Visit traditional villages and experience local life in the Chure region
  • Enjoy scenic views across the Terai plains and surrounding hills
Day by Day

Your itinerary

A suggested 3 day plan. Every departure can be tailored — talk to us about custom dates, pacing and add-ons.

  1. Day 1

    Kathmandu / Delhi to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi and travel to Dadeldhura

    Fly from Kathmandu to Dhangadhi. Travel from Delhi, arrival at border, cross border and night stay at town. End day with cultural dance and welcoming.

  2. Day 2

    Drive to foothills of Shivaliks (chure)

    Have breakfast and arrive at the foothills of Shivaliks. Hike for 4-5 hours through the jungle and arrive at the Chure (Shivalik) height and stay the night there.

  3. Day 3

    Descend from Chure (Shivalik)

    Have breakfast and then descend and arrive at the foothills and drive back to hotel. Night stay with another cultural event

  4. Day 4

    Head back

    Drop off to airport or drive back to your original destination.

  5. Opt Day

    Optional safari

    A jungle safari at Shuklaphanta Wildlife Reserve before you depart.

    6. Note:We recommend keeping one extra day in your itinerary as a buffer to accommodate unforeseen circumstances such as weather, road conditions, delays, or other unexpected changes.
