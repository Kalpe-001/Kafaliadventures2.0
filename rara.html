The Destination

A dive into the untouched far west

Known for its breathtaking alpine landscapes and the pristine waters of Rara Lake, Rara National Park is one of the most beautiful and peaceful destinations in western Nepal. Located in the remote Karnali region, the park is surrounded by dense coniferous forests, rolling hills, and snow-capped mountains, offering an unforgettable experience for trekkers and nature lovers.

Established in 1976, Rara National Park is Nepal's smallest national park and is home to the magnificent Rara Lake, the largest lake in Nepal. The park's diverse landscape supports a rich variety of wildlife, including Himalayan black bears, musk deer, Himalayan goral, red pandas, and numerous species of birds. The surrounding forests are filled with blue pine, spruce, fir, oak, and rhododendron..

Trip Highlights

  • Visit Nepal's largest and most beautiful alpine lake
