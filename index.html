About Kafali

Small groups, expert guides, unforgettable journeys.

Kafali Adventure is a locally rooted trekking and travel company built with one simple idea; to show the real Nepal, not the packaged version of it. We are based in Western Nepal, where mountains are quieter, trails are less crowded, and stories are deeper than just destinations. Every journey we design is guided by local knowledge, lived experience, and respect for the land we call home.

Our team consists of experienced local guides, mountain trekkers, and field experts who have spent years navigating the routes of Saipal, Api, Khaptad, Rara, and the remote Himalayan valleys.

  • Certified local guides
  • Small group sizes
  • 24/7 trail support
  • Sustainable travel
