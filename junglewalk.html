Jungle Walk

Explore Shuklaphanta's vast grasslands and forests on foot, discovering rare wildlife, peaceful wetlands, and the untamed natural beauty of Nepal's Far West

  • Duration 3 Days
  • Max Altitude ~1386 m
  • Region Sudurpaschim Province, West Nepal
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Best Season Oct- April
  • Price On Request
The Destination

A dive into the lush forest of Shuklaphanta

Shuklaphanta offers a completely different side of Nepal, where open grasslands, dense forests, wetlands, and quiet trails create an unforgettable wilderness experience. Located in the far west of Nepal, the reserve is renowned for its vast grasslands and diverse wildlife, making it an ideal destination for travellers looking to explore beyond the country's mountain trails. Over three days, the jungle walk takes you deep into this peaceful landscape, with opportunities to encounter wild animals and experience Nepal's rich natural diversity up close.

At Kafali Adventure, we believe the Shuklaphanta experience is about slowing down and letting the wilderness set the pace. Walking through the reserve allows travellers to appreciate its fragile ecosystems, observe wildlife in its natural habitat, and connect with the communities and landscapes of Far West Nepal. From expansive grasslands to quiet forest paths, every day brings a new perspective on Nepal's wild side.

Trip Highlights

  • Visit traditional Rana Tharu villages and enjoy cultural performances
  • Excellent bird watching opportunities
  • Guided jungle walk through Shuklaphanta Wildlife Reserve
  • Observe native wildlife like wold boars, swamp deers, tigers in their natural habitat
Day by Day

Your itinerary

A suggested 3 day plan. Every departure can be tailored — talk to us about custom dates, pacing and add-ons.

  1. Day 1

    Kathmandu / Delhi to Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi

    Night stay at Mahendranagar / Dhangadhi with short walk around the Tharu villages.

  2. Day 2.1

    Reach the wildlife reserve

    Have breakfast followed by departure and drop near Shuklaphanta wildlife reserve

  3. Day 2.2

    Jungle walk

    Guided half-day / full-day walking safari into the national park. Observe various floras and faunas along with scenic grassland views.

  4. Day 2.3

    Head back to town

    Arrive at checkpoint around 5-6pm and head towards the hotel. Dinner at 9pm followed by Tharu cultural dance.

  5. Day 3

    Departure

    Have breakfast at 7am and then head back to departure destination

    6. Note: One buffer day is included to allow for unforeseen circumstances such as weather delays and extra days can be added as per required for birding, observation or connecting with the nature and the wild
