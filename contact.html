Common Questions

Before you set out

How do I book a trek?

Send a request using the form above or visit our booking page. Our team will review your preferred route and dates and reply with the next steps.

Do I need to pay when submitting a booking request?

No payment is required to send an enquiry. We confirm availability and details with you first.

What is included in a trek?

Inclusions vary by route. We will share the itinerary, logistics, and what is covered when we discuss your trip.

What should I bring?

Bring comfortable clothing, suitable footwear, personal essentials, and any medication you need. Your guide will share route-specific guidance.

Are your treks suitable for beginners?

Some routes are suitable for beginners, while others require more experience. Tell us about your experience and we can help you choose.

Can you arrange private or customized trips?

Share what you have in mind with our team and we can discuss a route, dates, and logistics that fit your plans.

What happens if weather affects my trek?

Weather and trail conditions are considered when planning. Our local team will keep you informed and discuss practical options if conditions change.
